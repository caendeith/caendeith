<h2 align="center">🚀 Featured — The Broader Movement</h2>

<p align="center">
  <a href="https://github.com/caendeith/the_broader_movement" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">
    <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/caendeith/the_broader_movement/main/assets/banner.png" width="640" alt="The Broader Movement banner" />
  </a>
</p>

<p align="center"><b>Buy if you can · Broad if you can't · Pay, or Broad something of your own.</b></p>

<p align="center"><i>Not piracy. A promise between people who love culture and the people who make it.</i></p>

<p align="center">
  The Broader Movement is my flagship project: a solidarity protocol for cultural access. It reframes "piracy" as <b>Broading</b> — a social contract where a community decides that access to culture is a right, and everyone who can afford to pay, does.
</p>

| 🛒 **Buy** if you can | 🕊️ **Broad** if you can't | 🌱 **Pay, or Broad** your own |
| :---: | :---: | :---: |
| If you can afford it, buy it — your purchase keeps culture alive. | If you genuinely can't, receive it freely, without shame — and keep it flowing. | When things improve, settle the debt: pay the creators, or give back something of your own. |
